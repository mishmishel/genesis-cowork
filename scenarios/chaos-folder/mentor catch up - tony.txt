Meeting with Tony (Genesis mentor)
Coffee at The Grounds, 20 March

Tony's background: sold his SaaS company in 2022 for $12M, now angel investing and mentoring

Key advice:
- "You're building a vitamin, not a painkiller. Find the customers where this is a MUST-HAVE, not a nice-to-have. For Marcus (accounting practice), it's a painkiller. For the e-commerce people, it might just be a vitamin."
- "Your unit economics story is actually good. Most founders at your stage can't articulate LTV/CAC at all."
- "Stop trying to be everything to everyone. Pick accounting practices OR e-commerce. Not both. At least not yet."

On fundraising:
- "Don't raise until you have 20 paying customers and can show month-over-month growth"
- "Pre-seed in Sydney right now is $300K-$750K on $3-5M post-money. Don't let anyone tell you otherwise."
- "Talk to Airtree's explorer program, they back early-stage and have great founder support"
- "Start with angels who know your industry. VC comes later."
- Said he might put in $25K himself if we hit our Goal Night 2 milestones

On the product:
- Loved the mobile-first approach
- Said Xero integration should be #1 priority (same thing customers are saying)
- "Your free tier is too generous. You're training people to never pay."
- Suggested we add a usage limit to free tier to drive conversion

On Genesis:
- "Use the program. Most founders treat it as a checkbox. The ones who get the most out of it are the ones who actually implement the mentor feedback."
- "The finale is your chance to get in front of investors. Treat your pitch like a fundraise, not a school presentation."
