=== TOP DOWN ===

Total small businesses in Australia: 2.6 million (ABS 2024)
% that do their own bookkeeping/finance: ~60% = 1.56M
% in our target segments (accounting, professional services, trades, retail, e-commerce): ~40% = 624K
Assume $500/year average spend on financial tools
TAM (Australia) = $312M

But this feels wrong. Tony said top-down is lazy. Do bottom-up instead.

=== BOTTOM UP ===

Target: small businesses with 1-20 employees in Australia
Number of businesses in this range: ~2.3M (ABS)
Our addressable segments:
- Accounting practices: ~35,000
- Professional services: ~180,000
- Trades/services: ~370,000
- Retail: ~135,000
- E-commerce: ~65,000
Total addressable: ~785,000

Realistic penetration in 5 years: 2% = ~15,700 customers
At blended ARPU of $500/year = $7.85M ARR

If we expand to NZ: add ~30% = ~$10.2M ARR

SAM (5-year Australia + NZ): ~$10M
SOM (2-year, accounting practices only): 500 customers x $500/yr = $250K

=== NOTES ===
- These numbers feel small for a VC pitch. Tony said that's OK for pre-seed but we need to show the expansion path
- Expansion path: start with AU SMB, expand to NZ, then UK (similar regulatory environment)
- Also: platform play — if we own the SMB finance layer, we can add lending, insurance, payments
- Competitor A is doing $15M ARR with enterprise. Shows the market can support it at higher price points
