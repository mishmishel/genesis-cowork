## Part 3: Worker Not Chatbot

Here's the mental shift: **Cowork is a worker, not a chatbot.**

With regular Claude, you ask a question and get an answer. You copy-paste. You move on.

With Cowork, you describe an outcome, point to the files, and step away. Cowork plans, executes, organizes, creates, drafts — all while you grab coffee or take a meeting.

Think of it like this:
- **Regular Claude** = a researcher answering your questions
- **Claude Code** = a CTO writing code for you
- **Cowork** = a COO running your operational work

For founders, that means organizing files, cleaning up contact lists, drafting grant applications, researching investors, prepping for goal nights, building financial models — all without you copy-pasting anything.

Two quick housekeeping things: keep the app open while I'm working — minimise it, do other stuff, but don't close it. And any files I create stay in your folder after the session ends.

Now let me show you what this looks like in practice.
